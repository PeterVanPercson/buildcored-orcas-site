/ 08 — FAQ

Questions.
Short answers.

What is Buildcored?

+

A builder community started in Tashkent. People pick projects, build them, and put the code online. Most of the community lives on Telegram.

What is Orcas?

+

Our building program. v1.5 was 30 projects in 30 days, all software, shipped by 10 builders. v2.0 is the next one: in person, with real hardware, August 15–24 in Tashkent.

How much does it cost?

+

Nothing. The community is free and so are the programs.

How do I join?

+

The Telegram is open to everyone: t.me/buildcored. For Orcas v2.0, apply here by June 20.

Are the projects real?

+

Yes. All 30 v1.5 projects are in the gallery, each with the builder's name and a link to its GitHub repo. You can read every line of code.

What's the certificate?

+

Builders who finish a cohort get a certificate with a code on it. Anyone can check that a code is genuine at buildcored.com/verify.

Who runs Buildcored?

+

It's a social project by Husan Mavlonov, with help from the community and partners. Not a company. There's nothing to buy.
